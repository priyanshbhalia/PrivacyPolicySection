## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information you give us

- **Profile information:** any photo, details or preferences you add to your profile.
- **Content you submit:** messages, files, images, feedback or other content you create or upload.

### 1.2 Information collected automatically
- **Device information:** device model, operating system and version, unique device identifiers, app version and language settings.
- **Usage information:** the features you use, pages or screens you view, actions you take, and the date and time of your activity.
- **Log and crash data:** IP address, error reports and diagnostic data that help us fix problems.

### 1.3 Information collected with your permission
Some features ask for access to your device. We only access these after you grant permission, and you can turn them off at any time in your device settings:
- **Location:** to provide location-based features such as tracking or nearby services.
- **Camera and photos:** to let you take or upload pictures.
- **Notifications:** to send you alerts and updates.
- **Contacts, microphone or other sensors:** only if a feature you choose needs them.

If you refuse a permission, the related feature may not work, but the rest of the Service will.
